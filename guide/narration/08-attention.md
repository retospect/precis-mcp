# Attention

Not everything precis does can finish on its own. Needs-you is where
those things land: an open question, a hypothesis proposed for your
sign-off, a paper whose metadata automation gave up on. Every row here
is a real decision only you can make.

Alerts is the operational half of the same idea — open health
conditions raised automatically, cleared the moment the underlying
problem resolves, not just dismissed.

And if something in precis itself is broken, slow, or just wrong,
that's what gripes is for. File it right there in the app, in your own
words — it goes straight into the record the people building this
actually read.
