# Writing a paper

A draft in precis isn't typed, it's steered. You describe what you
want, and a writer works on it between your visits — your job is
reading what comes back and pointing it onward.

Open a draft and a fisheye table of contents sits on the left: every
heading, quiet stretches collapsed so a long draft fits on one screen.
The middle pane holds your focus — that's what a change request acts
on by default.

Pin another paragraph as context, without making it the target, then
describe the change in the request box and send it. Every citation is
a live object pointing at an actual passage, never text that can
drift from where it came from.
