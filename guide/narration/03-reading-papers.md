# Reading papers

Every paper — and every slide deck — opens in the same two pane
reader: a toolbox on the left, the source document on the right,
rendered as it was published. A citation preview from inside a draft
lands here too, so a claim's grounding is one click from the page it
came from.

The toolbox does the finding. Navigate searches the document's own
text, by meaning or exact keyword; Jump goes straight to a page, a
chunk, or an exact phrase. Sources and Cited show its bibliography and
everything that has since cited it.

It even works without a PDF: a stub still gets Navigate and Jump over
whatever text is already ingested, so you're never stuck waiting on a
fetch to start reading.
