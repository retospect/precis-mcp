# Figures and diagrams

A figure in a draft comes from one of two places: you upload an image,
or you draw one straight into the document with the model's help.
Either way, it travels with the draft into every export.

Upload a third party image and precis asks the one question that
matters: where did this come from. Original work and your own plotted
data are clear on sight; anything borrowed needs a permission record
before it saves — who granted it, on what terms. It's a ledger you
keep, so months later "who cleared this" is one click away.

Or skip that question and draw instead — a live canvas you build turn
by turn with the model. A figure drawn from your own data needs no
clearance, no expiry, and usually says what you mean better than the
one you'd have borrowed.
