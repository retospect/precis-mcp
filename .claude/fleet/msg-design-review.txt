Orchestrator instruction (Reto approved): design-bearing changes on this thread get the orchestrator's review before they land. Before you qland or land a change to a method, an algorithm, a construction, or a completeness claim, commit WIP and write a design note to {reviews}/{slug}.md: each claim numbered, the method, the evidence and how it was measured, what you are unsure of, and the commit range in your worktree. Then hold that item unlanded and carry on with work that does not depend on it. The verdict arrives in {reviews}/{slug}.review.md; poll for it. Mechanical fixes need no review.
